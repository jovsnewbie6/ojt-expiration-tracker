Quick Notes

  • Enter Year / Section as a single value, e.g. 3-3.
  • Submit once and use lookup to check review progress.

How it works

  1. Enter your name and year / section.
  2. Upload soft copies of your requirement documents if available.
  3. Click Submit Application to send your record for admin review.
  4. Click Lookup Status to view progress on existing submissions.
  5. If the admin marks a requirement as missing, a maroon alert shows the issue.
