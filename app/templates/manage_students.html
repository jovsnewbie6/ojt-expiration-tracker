Account Deactivation Policy

  • Soft Delete: Deactivating accounts removes access without deleting data—perfect for compliance and audit trails.
  • Prevents Login: Deactivated students will see an error message when attempting to log in.
  • Records Preserved: All submissions and documents remain in the system for historical records.
  • Reactivation: You can reactivate accounts at any time to restore access.
